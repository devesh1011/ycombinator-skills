# Y Combinator Program FAQ

Reference for questions about YC batches, the program, and how to interpret startup data.

---

## What is Y Combinator?

Y Combinator (YC) is a startup accelerator founded in 2005. Twice a year (now four times), YC invests $500,000 in a large group of startups. They work intensively with the companies for three months to refine their product, sharpen their pitch, and get funded by investors on Demo Day.

**Equity:** YC takes ~7% equity in exchange for the $500k investment.

**Structure:**
- 3-month program (the "batch")
- Weekly office hours with YC partners
- Group sessions with other founders
- Culminates in Demo Day — founders pitch to ~1,500 investors

## Batch System

### Naming Convention

Batches are named `{Season} {Year}`:
- **Winter**: January–March
- **Spring**: April–June (added 2025)
- **Summer**: June–August
- **Fall**: September–November (added 2024)

### Batch Timeline

| Era | Schedule |
|---|---|
| 2005–2023 | Winter + Summer only (2 batches/year) |
| 2024–present | 4 batches/year (Winter, Spring, Summer, Fall) |

## YC Program

### Application

Apply at `https://www.ycombinator.com/apply`. Any team size, any stage (idea to post-revenue). International teams welcome.

### Key Dates

- Applications open year-round for upcoming batches
- Late applications accepted with reduced odds
- Batch typically kicks off in the first month of the season

### During the Batch

- Founders relocate to the Bay Area (strongly encouraged but not required for remote-friendly batches)
- Weekly dinners and talks from past YC founders
- Office hours with YC partners
- Launch YC: internal platform for company data, hiring, etc.

### Post-Batch

- YC continues to support alumni companies forever
- Access to Bookface (internal forum for YC founders)
- YC Continuity fund for growth-stage investment
- Alumni network of 11,000+ founders

## Data Interpretation

### Company Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Operating, not acquired, not public |
| Inactive | No longer operating |
| Acquired | Bought by another company |
| Public | IPO completed |

### Top Companies

YC designates ~91 companies as "top companies" — generally the most valuable or impactful. These are highlighted on the YC website.

### Team Size

Self-reported employee count. May be outdated for older companies. Use as a rough proxy for scale, not precise headcount.

### Launched At

Unix timestamp of when the company was listed publicly on YC's directory. Not necessarily the founding date.

## Common Questions

**How many YC companies are there?**
~5,886 as of May 2026, across 49 batches.

**How many are still active?**
~4,051 (69%).

**How many have been acquired?**
~778 (13%).

**How many went public?**
23 (<1%).

**What is the YC acceptance rate?**
Historically 1.5–3% of applicants.

**Does YC invest in solo founders?**
Yes, though teams of 2-3 are most common.

**Does YC invest in non-profits?**
Yes. 42 YC companies are nonprofits (0.7%).

**Does YC invest internationally?**
Yes. YC startups span 100+ regions. Remote and international participation has increased significantly since 2020.
